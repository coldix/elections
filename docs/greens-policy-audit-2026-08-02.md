# Victorian Greens policy audit — 2 August 2026

This audit reviewed the Australian Greens Victoria across all 15 issues in the Victorian 2026 policy matrix.

## Result

- All 15 Greens cells already contained at least one sourced record.
- Thirteen policy files were updated.
- The federal immigration record was reviewed and retained because it already used current Australian Greens policy pages and clearly identified Commonwealth jurisdiction.
- The firearms record was reviewed and retained because it already used the current Victorian Greens gun-reform announcement and set out the party's ownership-cap, licensing, high-risk-firearm and buyback positions.
- Weak secondary sourcing was replaced with Victorian Greens policy, campaign and media-release pages.
- Standing policy aims are distinguished from dated 2026 election announcements and from measures the Greens have already secured in Parliament.

## Material additions

- Broadened cost-of-living coverage beyond rent to permanent free public transport and supermarket price-gouging regulation.
- Added the Greens' 100% renewable energy by 2030 target, public storage and transmission ownership, and household upgrade loans.
- Expanded education to include genuinely free public schooling, teacher conditions and public TAFE.
- Added justice reinvestment, bail support, the age of criminal responsibility and the Better Rights in Police Custody Bill.
- Replaced the integrity media summary with follow-the-dollar legislation, broader IBAC powers and political-transparency reforms.
- Added the proposed major-bank levy, wellbeing budget and public-financing position alongside the ultra-wealth property tax.
- Expanded climate, biodiversity and forestry coverage to include habitat laws, recovery plans, forest restoration and science-based fire management.
- Replaced Wikipedia and media Treaty sourcing with official Victorian Greens Treaty and First Nations policy.
- Rebuilt gender and social policy around reproductive leave, women's health, family-violence services, LGBTIQA+ safety and the Rainbow State Fund.
- Rebuilt health coverage around universal public care, mental-health clinicians, rehabilitation beds and free sexual-health services.
- Added affordable-housing definitions and planning requirements to the 2026 housing package.
- Expanded transport coverage to service frequency, active transport, electrification and public ownership.

The YAML policy records remain the source of truth. This note documents the audit scope and editorial method; it does not replace claim-level sources.

# Victorian Labor policy audit — 2 August 2026

This audit reviewed the Australian Labor Party (Victorian Branch) across all 15 issues in the Victorian 2026 policy matrix.

## Result

- All 15 Labor cells already contained at least one sourced record.
- Fourteen policy files were updated.
- The firearms record was reviewed and retained unchanged because it already used current Victorian Government primary sources and clearly set out the June 2026 reforms.
- Secondary media summaries were replaced with official Victorian Government, Victorian Budget, departmental or Commonwealth Government sources where suitable primary material was available.
- Records now distinguish more clearly between incumbent-government delivery, enacted law, funded Budget measures, stated positions and future commitments.

## Material corrections and additions

- Corrected the official cost of the half-price public transport measure to $278 million and the vehicle-registration rebate to $759 million.
- Added the Midday Power Saver offer, energy-storage targets and Labor's targeted gas-firming position.
- Updated federal Labor immigration settings to the 2026–27 permanent Migration Program.
- Expanded crime policy to include tougher bail, Adult Time for Violent Crime, the permanent Violence Reduction Unit and the sentencing-law review.
- Replaced secondary integrity, forestry, Treaty, housing, health and transport sources with current primary sources.
- Stated the Budget debt position without obscuring the distinction between rising nominal debt and the forecast debt-to-economy ratio.
- Expanded social policy beyond family-violence funding to include legal protections and LGBTIQA+ policy.

The YAML policy records remain the source of truth. This note documents the scope and editorial method of the audit; it does not replace the claim-level sources contained in those records.
